# Tessera UI direction

Reviewed 8 November 2026. The saved September research and reference screenshots remain in docs/research. Keep Tessera's name, cyan accent, dark palette, mark and sunburst. The saved Rescue flow and exact-path review are part of the shipped app.

## Window

Use one continuous window. A slim source sidebar, a large Explore or Rescue workspace and an optional inspector replace the fixed three-panel layout. Use quiet separators. Make the sidebar and inspector easy to hide. Do not reserve three columns at the minimum window size.

The map carries the color. Keep surrounding surfaces flat. Use system type styles, readable paths, visible keyboard focus and reduced motion. Avoid the small badges and repeated rounded cards in the old shell.

## Scan and review

Show the real scan phase, elapsed time, file and folder counts, current path and Cancel. New work is discovered during enumeration, so show an indeterminate progress bar. Never infer completion from file bytes. Name postscan analysis separately.

Explore keeps a large map, breadcrumbs and a compact list of largest items. Hover may explain a wedge. Selection owns all actions. Rescue stays a full workspace with its capacity target, coverage, ranked suggestions and proof.

Replace the large chip dock with a compact Cleanup List summary. Review opens a full sheet with exact paths, item removal and a stable total. Move to Trash remains explicit. Permanent deletion stays a separate confirmed action. Show moved, held and failed items, and distinguish moved bytes from measured free-space change.

## References

- Apple progress indicators: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/progress-indicators . Keep progress accurate and avoid a fast rise to 90 percent with a long tail.
- Apple sidebars: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars . Short groups and a hideable sidebar.
- Apple materials: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials . Glass belongs to controls and navigation.
- Apple accessibility: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility . Keyboard use, contrast and state beyond color.
- Apple macOS guidance: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-macos . Resizable windows and fewer nested levels.
- DaisyDisk: https://daisydiskapp.com/ . Let the map fill the window. Keep staging separate from removal. Saved screenshot: docs/research/tessera-ui-design-research-2026-09-01/references/daisydisk-scan.png.
- DaisyDisk deletion guide: https://web.daisydiskapp.com/guide/4/en/DeletingFiles . Its staging pattern is useful; Tessera retains Trash by default.
- GrandPerspective: https://grandperspectiv.sourceforge.net/screenshots.html . Dense data needs little chrome. Saved screenshot: docs/research/tessera-ui-design-research-2026-09-01/references/grandperspective-scan.png.
- AppCleaner: https://freemacsoft.net/appcleaner/ . One clear start action. Saved screenshot: docs/research/tessera-ui-design-research-2026-09-01/references/appcleaner.png.
- iStat Menus: https://bjango.com/mac/istatmenus/ . Give the main number priority and align secondary data in rows. Official screenshot: https://bjango.com/images/mac/istatmenus7/menus-2.avif . Reference only, not an app asset.

The design research agent checked primary sources and viewed the saved screenshots. Compilation does not prove visual quality. Native screenshots, resizing, input and partial-scan checks remain required.
